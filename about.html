About Fulfilled Hand Learning Centre

Our History

Founded in 2023 in Enugu, Fulfilled Hand started with a vision to help young Nigerians like you (Miracle) succeed in JAMB and tech. From a small tutorial with 10 students, we have grown to over 300 students both physical and online.


We are registered and recognized for excellence in teaching.

Why Students Should Enrol With Us?

  • Proven Results: 90% of our students score 250+ in JAMB
  • Tech Skills: We teach Coding, Web Design, Python
  • Career Guidance: We help you choose the right course
  • Flexible Learning: Morning, Afternoon & Weekend classes
  • Mentorship: Direct mentorship from top scorers
